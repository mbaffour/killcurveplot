Metrics

  • μmax — steepest slope of ln(signal); the exponential growth rate.
  • Doubling time — ln2 / μmax.
  • Lag — where the μmax tangent meets the starting value.
  • Lysis onset — first fall below the running peak by your threshold.
  • Log reduction — log₁₀(control / sample); needs a control sample.
  • Time to N-log drop — interpolated crossing, not the next sample time.

These are planning and analysis aids — always sanity-check against your raw data.
